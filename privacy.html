Privacy

Property Steward · updated 16 October 2026

Property Steward is a private tool for keeping track of maintenance, supplies and questions about a small group of properties. Only three people can use it, and nobody else can sign in.

Signing in with Google

When you sign in, Google tells Steward your name, email address and profile picture. Steward uses only your email address, to check that you are one of the people allowed in. It does not see or use your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar or anything else in your Google account.

After that check, Steward gives your device its own sign-in token so you stay signed in for 30 days. The token holds your email address and an expiry date, and lives only in your browser. Sign out, at the bottom of the app, removes it.

What you enter

  • Notes, photos and questions you type into Steward are sent to Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, so it can draft entries and answer questions. Door codes, combinations and passwords are removed from the text first.
  • Entries are saved to the property log, a Google Sheet, only when you press Save. A saved entry may note who added it.

What Steward doesn't do

No ads, no analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone else. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
